VisualTeX's pinyin input uses the dictionary and spelling rules of
万象拼音 (wanxiang), by amzxyz and contributors:

  https://github.com/amzxyz/rime_wanxiang (release v18.1.10, 2026-11-07,
  rime-wanxiang-base.zip, sha256
  ddebf0372a2a3d9d8f7a5017704b2afd7397a961c32be490a91e49ec3ee90999)

Shipped unchanged: dicts/zi, jichu, cuoyin, duoyin, diming and
mingren.dict.yaml (the dictionary tables) and wanxiang_algebra.yaml (the
spelling rules). Changes: VisualTeX's own schema (visualtex_pinyin.schema.yaml)
and dictionary manifest (visualtex_wanxiang.dict.yaml) use them without
wanxiang's Lua scripts, grammar model and other schemas, and leave out its
entertainers' table; the binary tables beside them (build/) are compiled from
them by RIME's rime_deployer.

The punctuation, key binding and symbol presets (punctuation.yaml,
key_bindings.yaml, symbols.yaml) come from rime-prelude (LGPL-3.0, the RIME
developers, https://github.com/rime/rime-prelude); the engine is librime
(BSD-3-Clause, https://github.com/rime/librime).
